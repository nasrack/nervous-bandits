Prototype rules v0.2

Players alternate one turn at a time. On your turn choose PUSH, HIDE, or RESCUE.

PUSH: draw 3. All Bandits immediately join the Camp. Choose one remaining card for your 5-card stash. If full, the new card may replace your weakest card.

HIDE: skip your draw and protect your stash from Bandits until your next turn. Two consecutive HIDEs end the round. Any PUSH or RESCUE resets the consecutive-HIDE count.

RESCUE: if the Hideout contains kidnapped cards, take one into your stash instead of drawing. The Hideout persists between rounds.

At 6 Courage, the Bandits raid the current leader. They kidnap that player's highest-value creature unless that player is hiding. If the leader is hiding, they raid the other player if possible. Courage then resets.

At round end, compare stash scores. The higher score earns the difference. The loser starts the next round. First to 15 wins.
