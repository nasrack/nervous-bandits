Prototype rules

Each round, players alternate turns. On a turn, draw three cards, choose one playable card for your five-card stash, then PUSH or STAY.

Builder creatures have values 1–5. Modifiers and tricks are intentionally simple. Nervous Bandits enter the central camp immediately. At 6 Courage, they rob the player with the higher current stash total by discarding one random stash card.

When both players STAY, compare final stash scores. The higher score earns the difference. First to 15 wins.
